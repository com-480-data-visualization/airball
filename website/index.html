◆ Act I
THE REVOLUTION

The three-point shot went from a gimmick to the foundation of the modern game. Click each era to see the story unfold.

1980

The NBA adopts the three-point line. Teams attempt fewer than 3 threes per game — coaches think it's a gimmick.

1994–97

The league temporarily shortens the arc to 22 ft. Three-point attempts spike, then collapse when the distance is restored.

2012

Analytics departments prove the three is the most efficient shot in basketball. Teams start listening — fast.

2016

The Golden State Warriors win 73 games on a record number of threes. Steph Curry rewires how every coach thinks about offense.

2024

Teams now attempt 35+ threes per game on average — 17× more than in 1980. The mid-range is nearly extinct.
