I The Revolution

A line drawn in 1980 redrew the sport.

When the NBA painted its three-point arc, nobody listened. Then analytics caught up. Today, NBA offenses bear almost no resemblance to the game played in the Magic-and-Bird era.

Data note: Team Stats Per Game and Team Summaries, NBA-only seasons from 1980-2026; shot geography combines Player Shooting zone shares with real NBA Stats shotdetail LOC_X/LOC_Y density for 1997, 2012, 2016, and 2026.

  1. Step 01
    1980

    The NBA adopts the three-point line. Teams attempt fewer than 3 threes per game — coaches think it's a gimmick.

  2. Step 02
    1994–97

    The league shortens the arc to 22 ft. Three-point attempts spike, then collapse when the distance is restored.

  3. Step 03
    2012

    Analytics departments prove the three is the most efficient shot in basketball. Teams start listening — fast.

  4. Step 04
    2016

    Golden State wins 73 games on a record number of threes. Steph Curry rewires how every coach thinks about offense.

  5. Step 05
    2024

    Teams attempt 35+ threes per game — 13× more than in 1980. The mid-range is nearly extinct.

Final takeaway

Basketball did not just get better at shooting threes; it changed how value, space, and greatness are measured.

Across five acts, the same pattern keeps surfacing: once the floor stretched, every other basketball question moved with it. Efficiency changed shot selection, spacing changed player value, and career impact became easier to see when era context entered the frame.
