# Screencast — voiceover script & shot list

**Target length:** 2:00 (hard cap)
**Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, MP4 H.264
**Tools:** QuickTime screen recording + iMovie/DaVinci for editing
**Voiceover:** one team member, clean USB mic, quiet room
**Music:** optional — low-volume cinematic loop (royalty-free, e.g. Pixabay "Inspiring Cinematic")

---

## Pre-record checklist

- [ ] Browser zoom at 100 %, window 1920×1080
- [ ] Cursor visible, no extension icons, no bookmarks bar
- [ ] Demo URL pre-loaded on `#hero`
- [ ] In Act II — pre-set year slider to 1982 for the play sequence
- [ ] In Act III — pre-loaded with LeBron vs Jordan defaults
- [ ] In Act IV — all six dynasties active by default
- [ ] Mute system notifications
- [ ] Practice cursor moves at half-speed; recordings always look faster than live

---

## Shot list & voiceover (220 words, ~110 wpm)

### 0:00–0:12 · Hero (12 s)
**Visual:** Slow zoom-in on the hero. Pause briefly on the title.
**VO:** *"The NBA today is not the NBA your grandparents watched. In 1980, teams attempted three threes per game. Today, they attempt thirty-five. We built Airball to make that transformation visible."*

### 0:12–0:32 · Act I — The Revolution (20 s)
**Visual:** Click into Act I. Click each story block in sequence (1980 → 2024) with a 1-second pause between each. Watch the orange highlight grow and annotations fade in.
**VO:** *"Act One traces the three-point revolution from a 1980 gimmick — through Daryl Morey's analytics era — to the Warriors making threes the league's foundation. Every story beat animates a new annotation on the chart."*

### 0:32–0:55 · Act II — Era Explorer (23 s)
**Visual:** Open Act II. Hit Play. Let it run from 1982 → 2026 (will take ~22 s at 500ms/year). Type "Curry" mid-playback to highlight his arc.
**VO:** *"Act Two is the sandbox. Every bubble is a player-season. Size is minutes played. We use D3 transitions, so the bubbles glide between years — you can literally watch the league morph. Search any player — like Curry — to follow their career through the eras."*

### 0:55–1:20 · Act III — Player vs Player (25 s)
**Visual:** Open Act III on the LeBron / Jordan default. Show the radar. Click "Raw stats" toggle, watch shapes morph. Click back to "Normalized". Type "Jokic" into player B and select.
**VO:** *"Act Three settles the cross-era debates. Raw stats are misleading — leagues evolve. Switch to Normalized, and every metric is a percentile against all NBA history. Now you can fairly ask: was Jordan more dominant in his era than Jokić is in his?"*

### 1:20–1:42 · Act IV — Dynasties (22 s)
**Visual:** Open Act IV. Hover over the Bulls' '96 peak — show the championship tooltip. Click off all teams except Warriors. Then click Bulls back on.
**VO:** *"Act Four is the team-level story. Win-percent trends over time, with gold diamonds marking championships. Toggle dynasties to compare windows — see how the Warriors' five-finals run lines up against the Bulls' two three-peats."*

### 1:42–2:00 · Outro (18 s)
**Visual:** Cut back to hero. Hold for 3 s. Fade in the GitHub URL and "Built by Elias, Michael, Yassine, Aziz · EPFL COM-480 Spring 2026" on screen.
**VO:** *"Built on D3, with seventy-eight years of NBA data. Four acts. One story. Try it yourself — link below."*

## Edit notes

- Cut every dead frame. If you say "uh" or pause, cut to a zoom.
- Use J-cuts (audio leads video) at every section transition.
- Add a thin gold underline animation under the act title at each scene change — consistency with the gold championship motif.
- Keep music at -20 dB under voiceover.
- Final 3 seconds: GitHub URL + team names on a black screen with the AIRBALL ◆ logo.
